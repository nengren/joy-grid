About Joy Grid

Joy Grid is a collection of free, browser-based puzzle games that you can play instantly — no signup, no downloads, no ads interrupting your gameplay. Just open a game and start playing.

We believe the best puzzle games are the ones that have stood the test of time. That's why we focus on classic grid-based games that are easy to learn, hard to master, and endlessly replayable.
