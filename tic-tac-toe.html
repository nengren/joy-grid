How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe (also known as Noughts and Crosses or Xs and Os) is a two-player game where you take turns marking spaces in a 3×3 grid. You play as X and the AI plays as O. The first player to get three marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row wins the game.

On Easy mode, the AI makes random moves — perfect for beginners. Medium mode uses basic strategy, blocking your winning moves while trying to win itself. On Impossible mode, the AI uses the minimax algorithm — it plays perfectly and will never lose. Your best result on Impossible is a draw!

Despite its simplicity, Tic Tac Toe teaches important strategic concepts: controlling the center, forcing your opponent into defensive moves, and setting up "forks" where you threaten to win in two directions at once. It's the perfect quick game for a mental break.
