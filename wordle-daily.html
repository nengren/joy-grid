About Daily Wordle

Every day, our Daily Wordle picks a single 5-letter word that all players around the world must guess. You get 6 attempts, and each guess reveals green, yellow, or gray feedback for every letter. The beauty of the daily format is the shared experience — you can compare results with friends without spoiling the answer, because everyone's solving the same puzzle.

Tips for Today's Puzzle

Start with a vowel-rich word to quickly identify which vowels are in today's answer. After your first guess, pay careful attention to yellow letters — they're confirmed to be in the word, you just need to find their correct position. By your third guess, you should have enough information to start making targeted guesses. Remember: common letters like E, A, R, T, S appear in the most English words, so prioritize those early.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Daily Wordle reset?

It resets at midnight in your local timezone. A new word is chosen each day, and it's the same for everyone worldwide.

Can I play Wordle more than once a day?

The daily puzzle is one attempt only. For unlimited replays, try Wordle Unlimited.

What do the colors mean?

Green = correct letter, correct position. Yellow = correct letter, wrong position. Gray = letter not in the word.
