How to Play Snake

Snake is one of the most beloved classic games ever created. Use arrow keys on desktop or the on-screen D-pad on mobile to guide your snake around the grid. Eat the orange food to score points and grow longer. Avoid crashing into the walls or your own tail!

The game gets progressively harder as your snake grows longer, leaving less room to maneuver. Plan your path ahead and avoid boxing yourself into corners. The best players think several moves ahead, like a game of chess on a moving board.

Choose your speed: Easy mode gives you plenty of time to react, Normal is the classic experience, and Fast mode is for players who want an adrenaline rush. Your high score is saved locally, so keep coming back to beat your personal best!
