How to Play Color Flood

Color Flood starts with a 14×14 grid filled with six random colors. You control the flood region starting from the top-left corner. Each turn, pick a color — your region absorbs all adjacent cells of that color, growing larger. The goal is to flood the entire board with a single color in 25 moves or fewer.

Look ahead before clicking. Count which color would absorb the most cells next. Sometimes it's better to pick a color that connects two large groups rather than the one that adds the most cells immediately. Think of it like building bridges between color islands. The best players can consistently clear the board in 18-20 moves.

Color Flood is deceptively simple — the rules take seconds to learn, but finding the optimal path requires real strategic thinking. Each board is randomly generated, so no two games are alike. It's perfect for a quick mental break that still engages your brain.
